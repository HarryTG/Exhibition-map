Floor-line points stay put (the floor slides a touch the other way) while the wall-top points follow your tilt, scaled by wall depth, so each wall stretches and leans like a model seen from above. Hold the device at a comfortable angle and press Recentre to set that as flat.

Some embedded previews block motion sensors. If the toggle falls back to drag, open the page directly on a phone over HTTPS for the real gyroscope.
